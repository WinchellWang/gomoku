Gomoku Arena is a lightweight browser game for quick matches on a classic 15 x 15 board.

## Game modes

- **Local PvP** lets two players share the same device.
- **PvE** puts you in control of Black. You move first against a strong computer opponent.

## How to play

Freestyle Gomoku rules are used: players alternate placing stones on empty intersections, and the first player to make an unbroken line of five stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.

The game runs entirely in your browser and can be installed as a PWA for a more app-like experience.
